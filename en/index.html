01 About

Business, management and technology

Hello, I'm Rui.

I combine business and management knowledge with technology, transforming data into relevant information to support more informed and rigorous decisions.

I hold a Master's degree in Business Intelligence and Analytics from ISCAP, a Postgraduate degree in Enterprise Information Systems Management from Porto Executive Academy, and a Bachelor's degree in Accounting and Administration from ISCAP. I am also a Certified Accountant registered with the OCC and hold a Pedagogical Skills Certificate.

I work as a Senior Technical Officer in Information Systems at Porto City Council, teach as a Guest Assistant Lecturer at ISCAP, and deliver training in Business Intelligence, data analysis and Artificial Intelligence applied to business.
